# Hallmarks of cancer and hallmarks of aging

## Эксперимент: Review synthesis of hallmarks of cancer and aging
**Тип:** Review synthesis  

**Последовательность:**

1. Reexamine hallmarks of cancer
   - Analyze the six hallmarks proposed by Hanahan and Weinberg in 2000 and their hierarchical arrangement.
2. Propose hierarchical model of cancer hallmarks
   - Arrange hallmarks from molecular to organismal levels, including molecular alterations, intracellular signaling, cellular resistance, tissue invasion, metastasis, and immortality.
3. Analyze hallmarks of aging
   - Evaluate the hallmarks of aging proposed by López-Otín et al. and identify shortcomings.
4. Propose hierarchical hallmarks of aging
   - Based on hyperfunction theory, arrange hallmarks from intracellular signaling to organismal age-related diseases.
5. Distinguish life-limiting vs non-life-limiting hallmarks
   - Apply criteria to identify which hallmarks are life-limiting and which are not.
6. Discuss aging as selective force for cancer
   - Explain how aging microenvironment selects for oncogenic mutations.
7. Identify common hallmarks of cancer, aging, and senescence
   - Highlight shared signaling pathways such as mTOR.


**Результаты (находки):**
- hallmarks of cancer hierarchy — Six hallmarks are genuine but not hierarchically arranged.
- hierarchical model of cancer hallmarks — Hallmarks can be arranged from molecular to organismal levels.
- hallmarks of aging — López-Otín hallmarks lack organismal level and include genetic instability based on refuted damage theory.
- hierarchical hallmarks of aging — Hyperfunction theory provides hierarchical hallmarks with age-related diseases as key organismal hallmark.
- life-limiting hallmarks — Only a few processes are life-limiting; non-limiting hallmarks are excluded.
- aging as selective force — Aging microenvironment selects for oncogenic mutations.
- common hallmarks — mTOR pathway is common to cancer, aging, and senescence.


## Гипотеза

- **Гипотеза:** Aging is caused by hyperfunctional signaling pathways (e.g., mTOR) that continue developmental programs, leading to age-related diseases; hallmarks of aging should be arranged hierarchically with age-related diseases as the key organismal hallmark.
  - Обоснование: If aging were caused by molecular damage accumulation, then reducing damage would extend lifespan, but interventions that reduce damage do not consistently extend lifespan; instead, inhibiting mTOR extends lifespan.

## Определение понятия

- **hyperfunction theory** — Aging is a continuation of developmental and reproductive programs that were not turned off, leading to hyperfunctional signaling.
- **geroconversion** — The process by which cell cycle arrest is followed by hypertrophic and hyperfunctional changes, leading to senescence.
- **life-limiting hallmark** — A process that actually limits lifespan in a particular individual, as opposed to non-life-limiting processes.

## Биологический механизм

- **Механизм:** Hyperfunction theory: Continuously active signaling pathways (e.g., mTOR) that drive development become hyperfunctional after growth completion, causing age-related diseases and aging.
